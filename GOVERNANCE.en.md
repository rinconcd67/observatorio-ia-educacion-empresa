## 1. Project Nature & Scope

The **Global AI Observatory for Education and Business** is an independent **Research, Development, and Innovation (R&D+i)** open-science initiative. 

The project operates autonomously to provide an auditable, reproducible, and public scientific monitoring system tracking artificial intelligence readiness, adoption, and governance across global regions.

---

## 2. Scientific Direction & Author Attribution

1. **Principal Investigator (PI):** Intellectual leadership, theoretical formulation, methodological architecture, indicator selection, and final approval of all releases, reports, and datasets belong exclusively and inalienably to **César David Rincón Godoy**.
2. **Academic Collaboration:** The project engages in scholarly collaboration with the Doctoral Program in Education and Artificial Intelligence at Broward International University (BIU) and international open science networks.
3. **Persistent Identifiers:** Every official version, software artifact, or curated dataset is archived and issued a permanent Digital Object Identifier (**DOI via Zenodo / CERN**) linked to the PI's ORCID record.

---

## 3. Core Research Lines

The Observatory structures its R&D activities across three strategic lines:

1. **Intelligent Agents & Self-Regulated Learning (SRL):** Investigating the impact of AI conversational coaches and agentic tutors on cognitive autonomy, user experience, and longitudinal persistence in higher education and professional training.
2. **Global Monitoring of AI Readiness & Governance:** Harmonizing international benchmarks (ECLAC/CEPAL, UNESCO, Stanford HAI, IMF AIPI, Oxford Insights) on national preparedness, talent readiness, and public policy.
3. **Telemetry, Analytics & Human-Centered AI (HCAI):** Engineering reproducible data pipelines, algorithmic audit frameworks, and predictive models for ethical and responsible AI adoption.

## 4. Technology Partnerships & Research Collaboration (Google & R&D Programs)

The Observatory actively welcomes strategic partnerships, research grants, and technology sponsorships (e.g., with **Google Research**, **Google Antigravity** initiatives, or cloud infrastructure grants):

* **Researcher Attribution:** Any technical support, computational grant, or co-developed workflow will ensure full scientific credit to the Principal Investigator in publications, case studies, and technical reports.
* **Editorial & Scientific Independence:** External sponsorship or computational assistance does not compromise the methodological integrity or findings of the Observatory.
* **Intellectual Property:** Scientific ownership of the methodology, datasets, and framework remains with the Principal Investigator.
