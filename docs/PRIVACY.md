# Privacy policy

The observatory does not request visitor accounts, passwords or payments. The newsletter presents a sample edition; subscriptions are not enabled yet.

## Page-view counter

The site includes an optional GoatCounter integration. Until the account is connected, measurement is disabled and the counter remains hidden. Once enabled, it records page loads only at the Observatory's public address, excluding local review copies. Totals start on activation and do not represent historical traffic or unique people. Public counts may take up to four hours to update.

The integration uses no cookies or local storage. It sends the page path without query parameters, fragments, title, email or referring URL. It respects the browser's Do Not Track signal. As the recipient of a web request, GoatCounter may process technical connection data under its own policy: https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy . The Observatory displays only aggregated totals and does not receive visitor IP addresses.

GitHub Pages and its associated network infrastructure may process technical logs under their own policies. External links lead to services governed by independent privacy policies.

Correspondence is initiated voluntarily by email and is not stored in the dashboard.
