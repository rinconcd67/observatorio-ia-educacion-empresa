## Author Note

César David Rincón Godoy is a doctoral candidate in Education and Artificial Intelligence at Broward International University in Miami, Florida. He holds a Master of Science in Virtual Education with a concentration in Neuroscience from Broward International University; master's degrees in Clinical Neuropsychology and Forensic Psychology from ISEP in Madrid; a master's degree in Government and Public Administration from the Complutense University of Madrid; and a master's degree in Clinical Psychology from the University of Lodz in Poland.

He serves as CEO of RINCONCD67 Consulting & Supply LLC, where he leads AI-driven business strategy consulting, corporate neuroeducation, and governance advisory services for energy and commodities operations. He brings more than 25 years of executive experience across the public and private sectors, including C-level leadership positions at PDVSA and participation in a World Bank project.

His doctoral research focuses on ethics and the logic of language in human-AI interaction, including the theoretical development of the Metaintelligence construct. He currently serves as an adjunct graduate faculty member at Broward International University in the areas of business strategy, artificial intelligence in education, and psychology.

The author reports no conflicts of interest.
